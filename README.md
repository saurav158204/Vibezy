# 🌐 Kindred — Find Your People

> Social discovery, reimagined.

## 💡 The Problem
People often struggle to find friends, communities, and activity partners
beyond their existing social circles.

## 🎯 Our Solution
Kindred connects people through **hobbies, shared interests, local activities,
and common goals**. Users can discover nearby communities, join interest-based
groups, and meet activity partners who share their passions.

## ✨ Planned Features
- 🧩 **Interest-based profiles**: pick hobbies and goals during onboarding
- 🤝 **Smart matching**: suggest people and groups with shared interests
- 📍 **Local discovery**: find hangouts, events, and activity partners nearby
- 👥 **Communities**: join groups like Travel Buddies, Fitness Crew, Music Lovers, and Gaming Squad
- 💬 **Group & 1:1 chat**: break the ice and plan meetups
- 🗓️ **Activity posts**: "Looking for a running partner Sunday 7 AM"
